ABOUT

Research. Technology. Global Impact.

My work combines electromagnetic theory, computational design, experimental validation and emerging data-driven methods to address practical engineering challenges.

My professional experience includes research engineering assignments at FCS Railenium and Université Gustave Eiffel in France, and a Visiting Professorship at THWS, Germany.
