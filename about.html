About Dr. Naveen Kumar

Academic researcher and engineer specialising in antenna engineering, RF and microwave technologies, and advanced communication systems.

Professional Profile

I am an academician, researcher and engineer specialising in antenna engineering, microwave technologies and advanced communication systems. My work combines electromagnetic theory, computational design, experimental validation and emerging data-driven methods to address practical engineering challenges.

I currently serve as Assistant Professor (Senior) in the School of Engineering and Technology at CHRIST (Deemed to be University), Bengaluru, India.

My research and professional experience spans academic and industrial environments in India and Europe.
